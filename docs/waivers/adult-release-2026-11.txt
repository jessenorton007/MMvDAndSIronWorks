2.  The Class
D&S teaches blacksmithing at its shop in Minersville, Utah. Participants heat metal in a propane
forge and shape it on an anvil with hammers, tongs, and other hand tools. An instructor supervises
all hands-on work. Participants must be able to stand at an anvil, hold hot metal with tongs, and
swing a hammer. Observers must stay where the instructor tells them to stand.
3.  The Risks
Blacksmithing involves fire, extreme heat, heavy tools, and hot metal. Some risks remain even when
everyone is careful. They include:
• burns from hot metal, flame, sparks, or equipment;
• eye injuries from sparks, scale, or flying bits of metal;
• cuts, bruises, and puncture wounds from tools or sharp edges;
• breathing smoke, fumes, or carbon monoxide from the propane forge or heated metal;
• heat exhaustion and dehydration;
• strains, sprains, and overexertion from hammering and lifting;
• trips, slips, and falls in a working shop;
• hearing damage from hammering and forge noise;
• fire, propane leaks, and tool or equipment failure; and
• injuries caused by other students or visitors, who share the anvils, tools, and work area.
These are not the only risks. Observers face many of the same risks, especially burns, eye injuries,
fumes, and noise.

4.  Safety Rules
D&S will give safety instruction before any hands-on work begins and will provide safety glasses
and earplugs. I agree to:
1. follow every instruction from the instructor;
2. wear safety glasses at all times in the work area;
3. bring and wear my own gloves, closed-toe leather shoes or boots, and long pants (natural
fibers such as cotton, wool, or denim are strongly recommended because synthetic fabric can
melt onto skin);
4. not touch the forge, tools, or hot metal without the instructor’s permission; and
5. tell the instructor before class about any health condition that could affect my safety.
D&S may remove anyone who does not follow these rules.
5.  Assumption of Risk
I understand the risks described in this form. I choose to take part in or observe the class anyway. I
accept those risks, and any others that are not listed, including risks caused by the ordinary
negligence of the Released Parties.
