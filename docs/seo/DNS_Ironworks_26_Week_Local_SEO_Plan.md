# DNS Ironworks 26-Week Local SEO Plan

**Prepared for:** Mojave Marketing
**Campaign:** $895 SEO Launch + Local Essentials at $159/week for 26 weeks
**Plan status:** Active internal plan. The technical SEO Launch implementation and Replit production routing are verified; account baselines and ongoing Local Essentials work remain.
**Planning principle:** One primary focused improvement per month. Priorities may change when reliable data shows a stronger opportunity.

**Current confirmed priorities (August 21, 2026):** 1. pre-made products; 2. custom designs/custom projects; 3. custom furniture. The primary local SEO target is **Custom Designs / Custom Projects**. Search Console is established, GA4 is active as `G-QW2GMHN0GZ`, and an existing GBP is in verification. Do not create duplicate properties or profiles.

## Scope guardrails

The one-time SEO Launch includes all initial assessment, setup, benchmarks, and agreed startup corrections. These items do **not** consume a Local Essentials monthly improvement. Local Essentials begins after that foundation and includes one focused website, content, technical, or local-visibility improvement per month, plus monitoring, GBP/review guidance, and summaries.

### Work allocated to the one-time SEO Launch

- Route-specific server-delivered metadata and canonicals.
- Correct 404 behavior.
- Preview-page redirect or removal.
- Admin protection and server-delivered noindex behavior.
- Preferred-domain redirect.
- Search Console verification/access and sitemap submission/setup.
- GA4 and initial conversion-tracking setup.
- Initial Google Business Profile existence/duplicate/ownership review and eligible setup after facts are confirmed.
- Visible Markdown corrections.
- Removal of unreliable Etsy cart-count copy.
- Initial technical, search, traffic, conversion, and GBP benchmarks with measurement documentation.

Separate approval is required for major architecture, multiple additional pages in one month, extensive citation cleanup, active backlink outreach, paid tools/directories, photography, advertising, legal work, or third-party fees.

## SEO Launch and baseline period

**Objective:** Make the site technically understandable to search engines and establish accurate business and measurement baselines.

**Planned work:**

- Confirm public name, eligible business location/service-area arrangement, priority services, hours, and target market.
- Use the existing Search Console property, connected GA4 tag, and existing GBP; obtain only the minimum reporting/configuration access still needed.
- Record indexed-page/query/traffic/conversion/GBP baselines only where access exists.
- Agree conversion definitions: form success, phone/text/email clicks, pre-made purchase intent, QuickBooks outbound click, and Etsy outbound click. Do not label outbound clicks as completed orders.
- Correct approved critical issues: route-specific crawl signals, real 404s, duplicate `/preview.html`, admin indexing control, preferred hostname, accurate sitemap/canonicals, and obvious content-format defects.
- Correct visible Markdown and remove unreliable Etsy cart-count copy.
- Review public and account-level evidence for an existing/duplicate GBP before recommending creation; set up only if eligibility and absence of a valid profile are confirmed.
- Validate with HTTP checks, rendered tests, schema tools, sitemap/robots checks, Search Console URL Inspection, and mobile review.

**Launch correction package:** Complete the listed technical, measurement, content-format, and initial local-profile foundation work. This package is one-time SEO Launch work, not a Local Essentials monthly improvement.

**Ongoing monitoring:** Uptime, robots/sitemap, status codes, indexed-page reports, security/manual-action notices, and forms.

**GBP/review guidance:** An existing profile has been created and verification started. Complete verification and duplicate/ownership checks for that profile; do not create another. Prepare accurate profile data and a no-incentive review process.

**Client responsibility:** Supply verified business facts; grant correct access; approve code/DNS/tag changes; identify primary jobs and real competitors.

**Dependency:** Website/deployment/DNS access, Google account ownership, and business facts.

**Deliverable:** Launch findings/correction log and baseline sheet.

**Measurement:** Successful status/canonical/schema tests; Search Console coverage and query baseline; GA4 acquisition/conversion baseline; GBP baseline if available.

**Decision point:** Select Month 1's service based on business value plus early query/index evidence. If access is delayed, proceed with verified technical work and documentation without inventing baselines.

## Month 1: Priority service and local relevance

**Objective:** Make the highest-value service page accurately answer a local commercial searcher's decision questions.

**Main monthly improvement:** Improve one existing priority service page with verified service-area language, process, project proof, qualification details, calls to action, and metadata. The likely candidates are custom ironwork, custom fire pits, or railings; the client/data chooses one.

**Planned work:** Map one primary intent; edit one page; improve headings/internal links/image text/schema only where supported; avoid thin city-page duplication.

**Ongoing monitoring:** Indexing, impressions, query relevance, engagement, and qualified leads to that page.

**GBP/review guidance:** Align the corresponding GBP service only if the profile is verified and the service is accurate.

**Client responsibility:** Confirm service priority, geography, project details, terminology, and photo permission.

**Dependency:** Verified facts and approval.

**Deliverable:** One improved service page and before/after record.

**Measurement:** Search Console page/query trends, GA4 landings/conversions, calls/forms attributed where possible; directional, not guaranteed.

**Decision point:** Continue with proof/content, or switch Month 2 if indexing/lead evidence identifies a more urgent issue.

## Month 2: Project proof and trust

**Objective:** Demonstrate local craftsmanship and reduce conversion uncertainty.

**Main monthly improvement:** Publish or substantially integrate one verified project story for the priority service, using real photos, problem/brief, work performed, materials/process, allowed location context, and outcome. Use an existing service page if a new page is not justified.

**Planned work:** Gather facts and permissions; optimize media; add internal links; use truthful alt text; avoid unsupported customer claims.

**Ongoing monitoring:** Organic entrances, assisted conversions, image visibility, and page engagement.

**GBP/review guidance:** Recommend one real project photo/update where appropriate; request a genuine review from the completed customer without incentive or pressure.

**Client responsibility:** Supply project facts/media and written publication permission.

**Dependency:** A suitable completed project.

**Deliverable:** One proof-led project enhancement.

**Measurement:** Search Console impressions/queries, GA4 engagement/conversions, and qualitative lead references.

**Decision point:** Use results to choose between local-profile improvement and technical/performance work next.

## Month 3: Ongoing local trust, GBP content, and genuine reviews

**Objective:** Strengthen ongoing local trust using accurate profile content, real project evidence, and a sustainable genuine-review process.

**Main monthly improvement:** Complete one focused local-trust improvement based on the established GBP foundation: verified profile content/project media plus a compliant genuine-review request and response process. Initial GBP review/setup remains SEO Launch work.

**Planned work:** Refresh one meaningful set of verified GBP content or local trust evidence, document the process, provide review-request/reply guidance, and avoid keyword stuffing, incentives, review gating, or duplicate profiles.

**Ongoing monitoring:** Profile status, edits, calls, website clicks, direction requests where applicable, and review activity.

**GBP/review guidance:** Activate a repeatable genuine-review request and response process using Google's review link/QR option; no incentives or review gating.

**Client responsibility:** Own the profile, complete verification steps, approve accurate public details, and send requests only to real customers.

**Dependency:** Eligible, verified GBP and client participation. If verification remains delayed, complete the month's local-trust improvement on the website using one approved set of genuine project/customer evidence; do not create a duplicate.

**Deliverable:** One focused GBP/local-trust content improvement and a documented genuine-review process, or the approved website trust substitute.

**Measurement:** GBP content/review/activity direction against the launch baseline, plus website trust-page engagement where applicable.

**Decision point:** Determine whether site discovery, trust, or performance is the next constraint.

## Month 4: Internal links and quote-request paths

**Objective:** Help visitors and crawlers move from important pages to the right service and a clear quote request.

**Main monthly improvement:** Improve internal linking and the route from important landing pages to requesting a quote across the homepage, services hub, one priority service cluster, and contact path without redesigning the entire site.

**Planned work:** Fix orphan/dead-end risks, use descriptive anchors, add relevant breadcrumbs if warranted, clarify quote CTAs, and keep canonical links consistent.

**Ongoing monitoring:** Crawl paths, landing-to-contact flow, outbound purchase clicks, and navigation behavior.

**GBP/review guidance:** Continue response guidance and monitor unauthorized profile edits.

**Client responsibility:** Approve customer journey and priority calls to action.

**Dependency:** Stable routes and conversion definitions.

**Deliverable:** Focused internal-link/conversion-path update.

**Measurement:** Crawl validation, GA4 path/event trends, and form/call intent.

**Decision point:** Confirm whether performance or a second service opportunity deserves Month 5.

## Month 5: Mobile media performance

**Objective:** Reduce avoidable mobile transfer and visual-load friction on high-value pages.

**Main monthly improvement:** Optimize the most impactful media/performance bottleneck supported by field/lab evidence, likely homepage video/image delivery and caching.

**Planned work:** Capture Lighthouse/CrUX/Search Console evidence; prioritize LCP asset, responsive images, video posters/lazy loading/compression, font/resource hints, or cache headers; change only the measured bottleneck.

**Ongoing monitoring:** Core Web Vitals, page errors, asset weight, and conversion stability.

**GBP/review guidance:** Maintain accurate photos and review responses; no separate content campaign this month.

**Client responsibility:** Approve visual compression/replacements and deployment changes.

**Dependency:** Reliable measurement and source media. CDN/hosting changes may require separate approval.

**Deliverable:** One measured performance improvement with before/after results.

**Measurement:** Mobile Lighthouse diagnostics plus available CrUX/Search Console CWV; use Google's good thresholds as direction, not a ranking guarantee.

**Decision point:** Choose Month 6 from query opportunity versus conversion friction.

## Month 6: Conversion and measurement refinement

**Objective:** Make organic progress measurable and improve the highest-friction qualified lead path.

**Main monthly improvement:** Refine the weakest measured conversion path or, if conversion paths are healthy or data is insufficient, strengthen the best-performing organic landing page using accumulated Search Console, GA4, and lead-quality evidence.

**Planned work:** Review event reliability, landing-page performance, and lead quality; select one measurable path/page; improve its form/CTA/content alignment; document the decision and exclusions. Initial event setup and false “order” corrections remain SEO Launch work.

**Ongoing monitoring:** Organic sessions/landings, qualified lead events, profile interactions, indexing, and technical health.

**GBP/review guidance:** Review six-month profile/review process and recommend the next sustainable cadence.

**Client responsibility:** Confirm lead quality and report offline outcomes that analytics cannot see.

**Dependency:** Enough event volume and feedback to identify a meaningful constraint.

**Deliverable:** One conversion/measurement improvement plus campaign evidence summary.

**Measurement:** Validated events and directional organic-to-lead trend; no revenue attribution where Etsy/QuickBooks cannot confirm completion.

**Decision point:** Select the next campaign phase based on evidence: another priority service, project proof, local visibility, performance, or conversion work.

## Final 26-week evaluation

**Objective:** Compare the end position with the launch baseline and define evidence-based next actions.

**Planned work:** Review technical health, index coverage, queries/clicks, organic landings, conversions, GBP visibility/actions/reviews, completed improvements, unresolved dependencies, and lead-quality feedback.

**Main evaluation:** Assess direction and quality of change, not guaranteed rankings or lead counts.

**Client responsibility:** Confirm lead outcomes, business changes, next priorities, and continued access.

**Deliverable:** Client-ready 26-week performance summary and prioritized next-phase recommendation.

**Measurement method:** Search Console, GA4, GBP performance, first-party form/click events, call data if available, and client-reported qualified leads. Each source is labeled by availability and limitation.

## Monthly reporting template

Each summary should state: work completed; evidence reviewed; primary improvement; technical/index changes; search visibility direction; organic landing/conversion direction; GBP/review status; limitations; client actions; and next decision. Report unavailable data as unavailable, never as zero.
